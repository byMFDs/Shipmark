# Community rules

These rules are part of the [Terms](/legal/terms/).

The community is where people who build with AI ask for help, show what they shipped, find collaborators and talk shop. Keep it useful for the next person who reads the thread.

## The short version {#short}

1. **Be kind and specific.** Criticise work, never people. Say what is wrong and how it could be better.
2. **Stay on the board's topic.** Off-topic talk goes to Off-topic.
3. **No spam or advertising.** Showing your own project is welcome in "Show your project"; posting the same link everywhere is not.
4. **No harassment, hate or threats.** No personal attacks, slurs or pile-ons.
5. **Nothing illegal or sexual.** No sexual content, no content involving minors, no malware, no stolen accounts, data or keys.
6. **Respect privacy.** Never post someone else's private information, email, address or messages without their consent.
7. **Only post what you may share.** Your own images and text, or things you have the right to post. Credit others.
8. **Do not pretend.** No impersonation, no fake proof, no claims that you wrote code you did not write.

## How posting works {#posting}

- New accounts can post 10 minutes after signing up. During the first 24 hours a post can hold one link and no images.
- There are limits per hour (threads, replies, upvotes, images) and the same text cannot be posted twice in a row.
- There are upvotes only. You cannot upvote your own post.
- You can edit your post. Edits in the first 15 minutes are silent; later ones show an "edited" mark. A thread's title can only be changed in those first 15 minutes.
- You can delete your post. If others already replied, their replies stay and yours shows as "[deleted]".
- Images are checked and re-encoded, and their metadata (such as camera location) is removed.

## Moderation {#moderation}

- Every thread and reply has a **Report** link. Reports go to the moderators; the person you report never sees who reported them.
- Moderators can remove a thread or reply, lock a thread (only moderators can reply) or pin it. They always write a reason, and the author sees it. Removed images are deleted at once.
- For repeated or serious breaks of these rules moderators can mute an account (read only) or ban it, as described in the [Terms](/legal/terms/).
- Think a decision was wrong? Write to [{{ contact_email }}](mailto:{{ contact_email }}).

## Reply notifications {#notifications}

When someone answers your thread or your reply, you see it under **Replies to you** in your account menu. We never send them by email. They are deleted after 90 days.
